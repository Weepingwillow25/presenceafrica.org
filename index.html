Coded Online Language

You choose what you see. Sensitive slang is veiled by default.

Online slang, emojis, and Swahili-Sheng phrases evolve fast across Kenyan social circles. While much of it is harmless humor, subtle coded language is frequently weaponized to bypass automated platform moderation and inflict technology-facilitated gender-based violence (TFGBV), harassment, and intimidation. This index helps you decode what is actually being said.
